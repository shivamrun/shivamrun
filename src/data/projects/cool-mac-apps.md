[Cool Mac Apps](https://coolmac.app) is an independent app-discovery platform built to help people find useful macOS software beyond the usual lists and storefront recommendations.

It combines a searchable app directory with structured product details, categories, editorial collections, and direct links to app developers. The focus is on helping visitors understand **what an app does and when it might be useful**, rather than simply collecting as many listings as possible.

## Why I built it

Finding good Mac software can be surprisingly fragmented. Interesting apps are spread across developers' websites, social posts, forums, and recommendation threads, while the information needed to compare them often varies from source to source.

I built Cool Mac Apps as a more focused discovery experience: a place where visitors can browse practical categories, quickly understand a product, and follow its official website when they want to learn more.

## What the platform includes

- A browsable and searchable directory of macOS apps
- Dedicated app pages with descriptions and official product links
- Practical product details, including categories, pricing models, developers, and hardware compatibility where available
- Categories for productivity, developer tools, utilities, design, screen recording, writing, and other workflows
- Editorial **curated lists** that group apps around a particular task or use case
- Featured listings and embeddable **Listed on Cool Mac Apps** and **Featured on Cool Mac Apps** badges
- A public app-submission form for suggesting new software
- A companion GitHub repository where people can suggest apps and contribute to the public list

## More than a directory

The individual app pages help visitors assess a single product, while the editorial lists answer broader questions such as which tools may help with research, file management, or screen recording.

That separation lets the platform serve both targeted searches and exploratory browsing without treating every app as a one-size-fits-all recommendation.

The editorial approach also has clear limits: being listed does not mean every app has been personally tested. Developers' official sites remain the authoritative source for changing details such as pricing and compatibility.

## Public platform stats

A public snapshot checked on **November 13, 2026** showed:

- **72 app listings** in the [directory](https://www.coolmac.app/apps).
- **22 browsing categories** on the [categories page](https://www.coolmac.app/categories).
- **5 editorial collections** in the [curated lists section](https://www.coolmac.app/curated).
- **2 embeddable badge types** for listed and featured apps on the [badges page](https://www.coolmac.app/badge).

These are live-catalog counts captured at the time of writing, not visitor, revenue, or traffic analytics. They will change as the catalog grows.

## Community and ongoing development

Cool Mac Apps accepts suggestions through its [submission page](https://www.coolmac.app/submit) and public [GitHub companion list](https://github.com/coolmacapp/cool-mac-apps).

The GitHub repository is an **open-source companion directory**, not a claim that the production website's code is open source. It provides an additional way for developers and Mac users to contribute useful discoveries while the main site continues to expand.
